About This Module

No data is transmitted to Instagram or any third party. There is no network call, no scraping and no model. The handle is hashed and used to select 4 of 10 pre-authored findings. The photographs are demonstration images stored on this server. This is a joke about photo-based diagnosis, built at a hackathon.
