A photograph is not a window into your hair color, your habits, or your health. This app will confidently pretend otherwise, and then — because it should not be trusted with a decision — it will hand four findings to the room and make everyone vote.

Nothing is fetched. No Instagram, no network call, no model. The handle picks four hard-coded findings out of ten. The photo below is a drawing.

The joke: you cannot diagnose a person from pictures, and you definitely cannot diagnose them from pictures they chose, lit, cropped and captioned. So the algorithm never gets the last word here — a room full of humans does. That's the only part of this we actually believe.
